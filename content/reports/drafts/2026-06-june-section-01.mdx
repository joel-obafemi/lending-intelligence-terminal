<SectionHeading number="01">Headline Finding</SectionHeading>

The June reading across Ethereum's six largest lending protocols is consolidation under yield compression. The Real Yield Spread between blended stablecoin lending and the 4-week U.S. Treasury bill closed June 30 at −37.1 basis points, a 36.8-basis-point deepening from the May 31 print and the second-deepest month-end inversion of 2026. Capital did not leave the sector in response. The six protocols absorbed $1.33 billion of net deposits at constant prices, with Aave V3's $845 million the largest single-protocol inflow in the captured series and Spark's $415 million a distant second. Five of the six saw positive flow. The exception was Euler V2, and its $21 million outflow was operational rather than depositor-driven, matching the same consolidation logic playing out inside Morpho. Morpho's curator concentration jumped from a Herfindahl-Hirschman index of 3,103 at May close to 4,557.8 at June close, the largest single-month shift in the captured series. Sentora, the largest Morpho curator at May 31 with 38.6 percent of curated TVL, now operates three vaults totaling $170 in assets. Steakhouse Financial absorbed most of that ground, growing from 34.1 to 59.5 percent of curated TVL. Issue 002's rotation thesis terminates. The June reading is that capital does not chase the marginal yield elsewhere when sector yields compress below the risk-free rate. It consolidates into the names already trusted.
