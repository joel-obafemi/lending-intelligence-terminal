<SectionHeading number="04" subsection="Euler V2" title="Euler V2" />

Euler V2's July constant-price net deposit flow was −$31M at the protocol level. That is the second-largest single-protocol contraction of the month after SparkLend, on the smallest available-liquidity base among the covered protocols. Available pool liquidity at July 31: $99M. Borrow book: $308M. Loan-to-deposit ratio: 74.86%, the highest among the six covered protocols.

The LDR reading above 70% is a function of the Euler Vault Kit (EVK) architecture, which allows individual vaults to be structured with high utilization targets. Euler V2's small available-liquidity base combined with a comparatively large borrow book produces high LDR, and the small denominator means each flow event moves the percentage share numbers meaningfully.

### Curator concentration

The Euler V2 vault surface is highly concentrated at the curator layer. Sentora runs 72.2% of Euler V2's curated TVL through five vaults. K3 Capital runs 14.3% through 13 vaults. The remaining share is distributed across a long tail of curators (roughly 12% of TVL held by curators the on-chain investigation could not resolve to a labeled name at the July 31 close). The combined V1+V2 HHI for Euler V2's curator layer is 5,512, which is materially higher than Morpho's 2,337 combined-view HHI. On a per-protocol basis, Euler V2 is more concentrated at the curator layer than Morpho, and the top curator (Sentora) commands a much larger share on Euler V2 than on Morpho.

### USDC supply APY

Euler V2's USDC supply APY at July 31 was 3.09%, the lowest among the four pool-based covered protocols. Combined with the −$31M protocol-level outflow, this puts Euler V2 in the position of being both the lowest-yielding and the most-contracting pool-based covered protocol in July.

### What August tests

Whether the Sentora-dominated curator concentration on Euler V2 holds or a competing curator gains material share. Whether the −$31M outflow continues or reverses. Whether the USDC supply APY closes any of the gap to Aave V3's 3.27% or drifts further behind. Whether K3 Capital's V2-heavy vault footprint expands.
