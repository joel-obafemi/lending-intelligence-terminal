<SectionHeading number="04" subsection="Fluid" title="Fluid" />

Fluid's July constant-price net deposit flow was +$26M at the protocol level. That is essentially flat: a small positive number in a month when Fluid's rate leadership on USDC narrowed materially, as covered in Section 02. This subsection covers what happened to Fluid's balance sheet under that rate compression.

Available pool liquidity at July 31: $464M. Borrow book: $571M. Loan-to-deposit ratio (borrowed as a share of total supplied deposits): 55.19%. That is above the 40.63% sector aggregate and second only to Euler V2's 74.86% among the six covered protocols. Fluid's above-sector LDR reading is a function of its smart lending and smart debt architecture: the same capital can serve as both lending liquidity and DEX liquidity through Fluid's cross-utilization design, which produces a higher effective borrow-to-deposit ratio than a pure Aave-style pool.

### Rate compression without corresponding outflow

Fluid's USDC supply APY fell from 6.41% at June 30 to 4.66% at July 31, a decline of 175 bps in a month. If Fluid ran on a pure yield-chasing depositor base, that kind of rate compression would typically produce a visible outflow. It did not. Fluid was net-positive on constant-price deposit flow in July, even with the rate premium over Aave V3 narrowing from 322 bps to 139 bps.

That is a data point about depositor stickiness on Fluid. The depositor base did not treat rate leadership erosion as a reason to leave. One interpretation is that Fluid's depositor pool skews toward participants who value the protocol's structural yield advantage across a cycle rather than chasing month-over-month rate changes. Another is that the July flow window (only 31 days after the June peak) is too short for a rate-reactive depositor base to fully rotate. August will resolve which reading is right.

### What August tests

Whether the +$26M flat outcome carries into August or turns negative if rates continue compressing. Whether Fluid's USDC supply APY stabilises around 4.6% to 4.7% or continues drifting toward Aave V3's 3.27%. Whether Fluid's LDR holds above 50% or contracts as borrow demand eases.
