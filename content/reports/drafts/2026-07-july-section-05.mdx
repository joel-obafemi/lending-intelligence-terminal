<SectionHeading number="05" title="What August Tests" />

Issue 004 documents a sector at half speed. Growth halved from June, concentration on the curator layer climbed for a second consecutive month, the real-yield deficit deepened, and one protocol carried most of the aggregate flow. Whether these are trend prints or one-month artifacts is what August resolves. The specific tests, grouped by decisiveness:

### The pattern tests

**Does one protocol continue to carry the sector?** July's aggregate of +$553M was Aave V3 plus a small remainder. Two consecutive months of Aave-dominant growth (June +$845M, July +$649M) is not a base case for a healthy multi-protocol sector. August's per-protocol flow numbers decide whether concentration in flow is now a three-month trend or reverts as depositors return to a broader set of venues.

**Does curator concentration keep building?** Morpho combined V1+V2 HHI moved from 2,095 at June 30 to 2,337 at July 31. Sentora and Steakhouse together added 447 bps of share coincidentally. A third consecutive month of climbing HHI hardens the two-curator dominance framing. Even a modest reversal shifts the read to "consolidation had a peak in July."

**Does the real-yield deficit deepen further?** RYS went from −37 bps in June to −75 bps in July. Sector-wide stablecoin lending now pays less than a T-bill in every pool-based market. Another 30+ bps deepening in August pushes the deficit toward levels not seen since March. A reversal would suggest the July compression was cyclical rather than structural.

### The protocol-specific tests

**Aave V3:** whether the LRT rotation (weETH in, rsETH out) continues; whether the USDe-complex stablecoin composition shift accelerates; whether the USDC drain resumes at June's magnitude or holds at July's slower pace.

**SparkLend:** whether depositor outflow following the July 23 Sky rate cut continues or stabilises; whether another Atlas Edit adjustment moves the Base Rate margin again; whether the unattributed July 12 outflow event finds a public cause after the fact.

**Morpho:** whether V2 share of curated TVL pushes above 80% or plateaus in the 70-75% range; whether Midnight's first full post-launch month produces observable maturity-bucket clustering; whether Gauntlet's V1-heavy footprint reverses as migration continues.

**Fluid:** whether depositor stickiness holds under continued rate compression, or the +$26M flat July becomes a negative August; whether USDC supply APY stabilises around 4.6% or drifts further toward Aave V3's 3.27%.

**Compound V3 and Euler V2:** whether the modest July outflows continue or reverse; whether either protocol shows signs of gaining ground on the pool-based rate front, where both currently sit at the low end of the sector.

### What would change the read

A month in which Aave V3 goes flat or negative while another protocol absorbs material inflow would falsify the concentration-in-flow trend and force a re-read of what July's pattern actually meant. Without that, the base case for Issue 005 is: same shape, deeper. If concentration and compression climb for a third consecutive month, the sector is in a phase where the winners have already won and the depositor pool is thinning. If either pattern reverses, Issue 005 gets a story about what changed.
