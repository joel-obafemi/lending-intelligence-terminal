<SectionHeading number="04" subsection="Compound V3" title="Compound V3" />

Compound V3's July constant-price net deposit flow was −$23M at the protocol level. That is a modest contraction. Available pool liquidity at July 31: $908M. Borrow book: $460M. Loan-to-deposit ratio: 33.48%, below the sector's 40.63% aggregate.

Compound V3 currently runs six Comet markets on Ethereum, one per base asset: USDC, USDT, WETH, wstETH, WBTC, and USDS. The cWETHv3 base (WETH-denominated) held $94.87M in supply at July 31.

### USDC supply APY

Compound V3's USDC supply APY at July 31 was 3.19%, at the low end of the four pool-based covered protocols. Aave V3 sat at 3.27%, Fluid at 4.66%, Euler V2 at 3.09%. Compound V3's rate is only 10 bps above the sector's laggard (Euler V2) and 8 bps below Aave V3. That is not a rate-competitive position for a protocol that historically led on USDC yields in earlier cycles.

The Comet architecture (isolated per-base pools with each collateral asset priced against a single borrowable base) trades cross-collateral efficiency for market simplicity. On a month when the sector's stablecoin lending competition is compressing toward the T-bill floor, that trade-off is producing modest outflows rather than growth.

### What August tests

Whether the modest July outflow continues or reverses. Whether the cWETHv3 base grows meaningfully above $94.87M or plateaus. Whether the two newer Comet bases (wstETH and WBTC) show any material activity, which would speak to whether Compound V3's multi-base expansion is finding users beyond the two originals.
